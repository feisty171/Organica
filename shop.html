JUVE

JUVE is a biomimetic serum built around six named actives, chosen to work with the skin's own processes rather than force a result. Peptides, soluble collagen, and hyaluronic acid sit alongside panthenol for comfort, in a formula meant to be checked ingredient by ingredient, not taken on faith.

Two pumps at night, before anything else. One bottle carries roughly a month at that pace, which is why the range is sold by the bottle and by the case rather than as a single fixed size.

JUVE Biomimetic Stem Cell Activator — formulated in Singapore, six actives, one nightly step.

Ingredients

Six actives, named in full.

+

Renewal

Oligopeptide 24

A signalling peptide included to support the skin's own renewal process, contributing to a smoother, more even surface over time.

INCI: Oligopeptide-24

+

Firmness

Matrixyl

A well known peptide studied for how it works alongside skin's own collagen signalling, supporting the look of firmness and density with continued use.

INCI: Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4

Expression lines

Argireline

A neurocosmetic peptide designed to ease tension at the surface, softening the look of expression lines around the eyes and forehead.

INCI: Acetyl Hexapeptide-3

+

Hydration

Hyaluronic Acid

A moisture binding molecule that draws and holds water at the skin's surface, supporting a plumper, more hydrated feel.

INCI: Sodium Hyaluronate

+

Surface plumpness

Soluble Collagen

Hydrolysed collagen sized to sit on the skin's surface, helping it hold moisture and look fuller.

INCI: Soluble Collagen

+

Comfort

Panthenol

Pro vitamin B5, included to support the skin barrier and leave a calm, conditioned feel after application.

INCI: Panthenol

Bright Mask

Bright+ is a fibre silk sheet mask built around alpha-arbutin and an antioxidant complex, designed to help brighten the look of skin and leave it feeling smoother and more hydrated after each use.

The sheet itself is spun from natural silk protein rather than a synthetic fibre, chosen for how closely it sits against the skin for the 15 to 20 minutes it's left on.

Bright+ Fibre Silk Mask — formulated in Singapore, worn for 15 to 20 minutes, used 2 to 3 times a week.

Ingredients

Four actives, worn against the skin.

+

Brightening

Alpha-Arbutin

Works by limiting the enzyme responsible for excess pigment production, helping even out the look of skin tone with regular use.

INCI: Alpha-Arbutin

+

Even tone

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3, included to help even out the look of skin tone, support the skin barrier, and keep pores looking refined.

INCI: Niacinamide

+

Protection

LumiFullerene Complex

A carbon based antioxidant complex included to help defend skin against everyday environmental stress.

INCI: Fullerene (C60)

+

Comfort & fit

Silk Fibre

A natural fibre chosen for how closely it follows the contours of the face, helping the essence stay in contact with skin for the full application.

Material: Natural silk protein (fibroin)
