ProBio Prepé

Prepé is a microbiome-focused mousse face cleanser designed to remove makeup and daily buildup while supporting the skin barrier. Its light foam is suitable for all skin types.

The formula pairs Bacillus coagulans and DEFENSIL®-PURE ECO with lactic acid and botanical extracts to cleanse and refine texture while leaving skin feeling fresh rather than stripped.

Product description
Net Weight120ml
Recommend UsageUse daily. Day and night
Formulated inGermany
Made inSingapore
The actives

Bacillus Coagulans — supports the skin's microbial balance and natural barrier.

DEFENSIL®-PURE ECO — included to soothe the feel of irritated skin and support a balanced microbiota.

Lactic Acid (AHA) — gently refines texture and helps skin look smoother, brighter and more even.

Comfrey, Witch Hazel and Licorice Root Extracts — a botanical blend chosen to soothe, refine the look of pores and support a more even-looking tone.

Full ingredient list is printed on the pack. Prepé is a cosmetic product; individual results vary.

How to use it

Dispense onto damp palms and work into a light lather. Massage gently over the face for 30 to 60 seconds, avoiding the eye area, then rinse thoroughly. Use morning and evening.
