FAQ

The honest version.

How is commission calculated?
You earn a fixed percentage of the order subtotal on every purchase made with your code. The rate is the same for every affiliate — it doesn't change based on volume, tenure, or how many people you've referred.
When do payouts happen?
Commission is confirmed once each order is finalized, then paid out on a set schedule. PLACEHOLDER — exact payout dates and method to confirm before publishing.
Are there recruitment requirements or tiers?
No. There's nothing to recruit, no downline, and no ranks to climb. Every affiliate earns the same flat rate on every order — whether it's their first or their fiftieth. This is a commission program, not a structure you build under yourself.
