Orchid Idebenone Elixir Glow

Orchid Idebenone Elixir Glow is a luxurious antioxidant facial elixir designed to nourish, protect and restore the skin’s natural luminosity. Powered by Idebenone and oil-soluble Vitamin C, this concentrated formula helps defend against environmental oxidative stress while improving the appearance of dullness, dryness and visible signs of ageing.

Enriched with Camellia Japonica and Meadowfoam Seed Oils, its silky texture replenishes essential moisture and supports a smoother, softer and more resilient complexion. Skin appears supple, revitalised and illuminated with a healthy, youthful glow.
