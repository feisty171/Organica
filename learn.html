How to use it

One step, in the right order.

  1. Cleanse

    Start on clean, dry skin.

  2. Two pumps

    Press twice into the palm and press into face and neck rather than rubbing.

  3. Wait a minute

    Let it settle before anything goes on top. This is the step most people skip.

  4. Seal

    Follow with your moisturiser. Nightly, every night, for as long as the course runs.
