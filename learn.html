FAQ

Questions by product.

How should I use the JUVE facial mist?
After cleansing, spray evenly over the face from approximately 20cm away. It may be used under or over makeup.
What are the six JUVE power actives?
Oligopeptide-24, Matrixyl®, Argireline®, soluble collagen, hyaluronic acid and panthenol.
How long should Bright+ stay on the skin?
Leave the fibre silk mask on for 15 to 20 minutes, then remove it and gently massage in the remaining essence.
What does Bright+ target?
The formula is designed to support brighter, smoother and more hydrated-looking skin while helping refine the appearance of pores and uneven tone.
Is ProBio Prepé suitable for daily use?
Yes. It is designed as a light mousse cleanser for morning and evening use and is suitable for all skin types.
How do I use ProBio Prepé?
Work the cleanser into a light lather on damp palms, massage over the face for 30 to 60 seconds, avoid the eye area, and rinse thoroughly.
