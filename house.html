About Us

Our Heritage

Organica is the skincare division of Mary Chia Group, shaped by more than four decades of beauty and wellness expertise. From a founder-led Singapore business to a growing regional community, each chapter has deepened our understanding of skin and guided the purposeful formulations we create today. Begin the journey below to discover the milestones, people and ambition behind Organica.

The Beginning

1982

The Beginning

Mary Chia’s story began with a simple yet meaningful vision: to make professional beauty care more personal and accessible. Founded by Madam Mary Chia, the business provided customised beauty treatments through doorstep services, building close and trusted relationships with its customers. This personal approach to beauty and wellness became the foundation of Mary Chia.

1989

The First Beauty and Slimming Centre

Building upon the trust established with her customers, Madam Mary Chia opened the first physical beauty and slimming centre in Singapore. The opening marked an important transition from a home-based entrepreneurial venture into an established beauty business, creating a dedicated space where customers could experience professional treatments and personalised care.

1994

Official Incorporation

Mary Chia Beauty & Slimming began formally incorporating in Singapore, providing the business with a stronger corporate foundation for its next stage of development. This milestone marked the evolution of Mary Chia from a founder-led beauty centre into a professionally managed beauty and wellness company with the structure and ambition to grow.

2001–2004

Expanding the Brand’s Reach

As Mary Chia entered a new decade, the company strengthened its visibility through the appointment of its first celebrity brand ambassador, renowned Singapore actress Ivy Lee. In 2003, the Group expanded into the men’s wellness market with the launch of Urban Homme, one of Singapore’s pioneering wellness centres created specifically for men. This was followed by the Singapore Superbrands Award in 2004, recognising Mary Chia as one of the country’s leading consumer brands.

2007

Recognition for Brand Excellence

Mary Chia received the Singapore Prestige Brand Award in recognition of its achievements in brand development, business growth and customer confidence. The award reflected the company’s successful evolution from a local beauty centre into an established Singapore brand with a growing portfolio of beauty, slimming and wellness services.

2009

Listed on the Singapore Exchange

Mary Chia Holdings Limited reached a defining corporate milestone with its successful listing on the Singapore Exchange. The listing represented a new stage of corporate maturity, creating a stronger platform for business development, brand expansion and long-term growth while reinforcing the Group’s position within Singapore’s beauty and wellness industry.

2010–2011

Growing Regional Recognition

Mary Chia strengthened its presence across Asia through the appointment of internationally renowned actress Vicki Zhao Wei as its brand ambassador. In 2011, the Group brought thousands of participants together for a landmark beauty event that achieved a Guinness World Record. The achievement demonstrated the strength of the Mary Chia community and the brand’s growing leadership within the beauty and wellness industry.

2013–2016

Excellence in Service and Leadership

Beyond commercial growth, Mary Chia expanded its community impact through charitable initiatives and strategic partnerships designed to inspire confidence and connection. The Group subsequently received recognition for service excellence, strong customer experiences and business leadership, including the Singapore Service Class accreditation and the ASEAN Outstanding Business Award.

2018–2019

A Singapore Heritage Brand

Mary Chia further strengthened customer confidence by attaining internationally recognised quality and service standards. In 2019, the company was recognised as a Singapore Heritage Brand and honoured with the Singapore Entrepreneurship Award. These achievements celebrated the enduring legacy of Madam Mary Chia’s entrepreneurial journey and the brand’s contribution to generations of customers.

2020–2022

Evolving for a New Generation

The Group entered a new chapter of transformation with a refreshed Mary Chia identity and a renewed focus on delivering more contemporary, customer-centric experiences. Its professional education capabilities were strengthened through Spa Menu Academy’s attainment of its Approved Training Organisation licence, while partnerships with leading retail and e-commerce platforms extended the accessibility of the Group’s products beyond its physical outlets.

2024–2026

Organica International Rises Strong

The reactivation of Organica International in Singapore marked an important step in the Group’s regionalisation strategy. Drawing upon Mary Chia Group’s decades of beauty and wellness expertise, Organica introduced a new platform for innovative skincare, community development and entrepreneurship. The brand subsequently expanded into key Asian markets, connecting a growing regional community through shared products, knowledge and opportunities.

Our Vision & Mission

The principles that shape what we formulate, how we grow and who we grow with.

01

Mission

Organica exists to remind every person that beauty is not something you earn, it is something you already carry. We believe every body, every skin, every stage of life deserves to feel confident, cared for and radiant. That belief is why we formulate with real science and biomimetic technology, so that the promise of youth is not a marketing line but something you can feel in your skin with every use. And because confidence should not stop at the mirror, we are building a business around Organica where the people who believe in it, our partners, our creators, our community, can turn that belief into their own income and independence. Beauty that is honest. Products that work. Opportunity that is real. That is what we are building, together.

02

Vision

We see Organica becoming one of Asia Pacific's most trusted names in modern skincare, not because we shout the loudest, but because we deliver quietly and consistently on what we promise. We envision a future where feeling beautiful and being financially free are not two separate dreams but one shared journey, where the same community that trusts our products also grows alongside us, building livelihoods and businesses of their own. Organica is not just a skincare brand. It is a place where confidence, integrity and opportunity meet, and where everyone who joins us has the chance to feel a little more beautiful and a little more free.
