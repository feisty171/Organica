The House

Our story.

Organica is the skincare arm of Mary Chia Group. We formulate our own products rather than relabelling someone else's, and we have four decades of the parent company's experience behind that decision.

1982

Mary Chia begins

Today

Where we are now.

A small range, kept small enough to explain in full, product by product. Three things we hold to on every one of them:

01

Biomimetic by design

Ingredients built to resemble molecules the skin already recognises, chosen for compatibility rather than force.

02

Named in full

Every active is listed with its INCI name. Nothing hides behind a trademark you cannot look up.

03

Checked, not just claimed

If a claim would not hold up to a dermatologist asking a direct question, it does not go on the label.

2024

JUVE launches.

Ten years of formulating leads to Organica's current flagship — six named actives, one nightly step, nothing hidden behind a trademark.

2014

Organica is founded.

The Group's dedicated skincare arm — formulating rather than reselling, for skin and climate closer to home than most imported brands bother with.

2009

Public listing.

Mary Chia Group lists on the Singapore Exchange — SGX Catalist. What started with one client at a time is now audited, reported, and answerable to shareholders.

1989

A real address.

Seven years in, the door-to-door round became a beauty and slimming centre at Goldhill Centre. Four staff, including Mary and her eldest daughter, Wendy.

1982

Where it started.

Mary Chia began by going door to door, offering facials to whoever would let her in. No shopfront, no backers — just a service people kept asking for again. Everything above traces back to that.

What drives us

Our Vision & Mission

Mission

Organica exists to remind every person that beauty is not something you earn, it is something you already carry. We believe every body, every skin, every stage of life deserves to feel confident, cared for and radiant. That belief is why we formulate with real science and biomimetic technology, so that the promise of youth is not a marketing line but something you can feel in your skin with every use. And because confidence should not stop at the mirror, we are building a business around Organica where the people who believe in it, our partners, our creators, our community, can turn that belief into their own income and independence. Beauty that is honest. Products that work. Opportunity that is real. That is what we are building, together.

Vision

We see Organica becoming one of Asia Pacific's most trusted names in modern skincare, not because we shout the loudest, but because we deliver quietly and consistently on what we promise. We envision a future where feeling beautiful and being financially free are not two separate dreams but one shared journey, where the same community that trusts our products also grows alongside us, building livelihoods and businesses of their own. Organica is not just a skincare brand. It is a place where confidence, integrity and opportunity meet, and where everyone who joins us has the chance to feel a little more beautiful and a little more free.
