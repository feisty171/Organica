The actives

Oligopeptide-24 - The Renewal Signal — a bioactive signalling peptide that supports the skin's natural renewal process, helping refine texture and promote a smoother, healthier-looking complexion.

Matrixyl® (Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4) - The Firming Architect — supports collagen-related pathways, helping improve the appearance of firmness, elasticity and skin density for a more youthful-looking structure.

Argireline® (Acetyl Hexapeptide-8) - The Expression-Line Softener — helps soften the appearance of expression lines caused by repetitive facial movement, particularly around the forehead and eye area.

Soluble Collagen - The Plumping Veil — a moisture-supporting collagen ingredient that conditions the skin's surface, helping it appear smoother, softer and visibly fuller.

Hyaluronic Acid - The Moisture Magnet — attracts and retains moisture, delivering an immediate plumping effect while keeping skin supple and comfortably hydrated.

Panthenol - The Barrier Guardian — Pro-Vitamin B5 helps comfort and condition the skin while supporting its moisture barrier, leaving it feeling calmer, softer and more resilient.

Full ingredient list is printed on the pack. JUVE is a cosmetic product; individual results vary.

How to use it

Apply JUVE Biomimetic Stem Cell Activator after cleansing. Spray evenly onto the face, followed by your forehead, maintaining a distance of approximately 20cm from the skin. Spray under or over makeup for a brighter, lifted finish.
