Bright+ Fibre Silk Mask

The Bright+ Fibre Silk Mask is a luxurious treatment mask infused with C60 LumiFullerene Complex, Alpha-Arbutin, Niacinamide, Panthenol and Sodium Hyaluronate.

Designed to illuminate, hydrate and rejuvenate, it delivers exceptional antioxidant power for radiant, youthful-looking skin.

Product description
Net Weight25gm * 5pcs
Recommend UsageDaily - Alternate day
Formulated inUSA
Made inSingapore
The actives

C60 LumiFullerene Complex — provides antioxidant support against environmental stress.

Alpha-Arbutin and Niacinamide — help brighten the look of skin, refine pores and promote a more even-looking tone.

Panthenol and Sodium Hyaluronate — soothe, moisturise and help strengthen the skin's moisture barrier.

Silk Fibre — conforms closely to the face for effective absorption and comfortable wear.

Full ingredient list is printed on the pack. Bright Mask is a cosmetic product; individual results vary.

How to use it

Gently remove the fibre silk mask from its packaging and unfold it. Place it onto the face, aligning with the eyes, nose and mouth. Leave on for 15 to 20 minutes, remove, and gently massage any remaining essence into the skin. Follow with your regular skincare routine.
