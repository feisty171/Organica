Bright Mask

—

Bright+ is a fibre silk sheet mask built around alpha-arbutin and an antioxidant complex, designed to help brighten the look of skin and leave it feeling smoother and more hydrated after each use.

The sheet itself is spun from natural silk protein rather than a synthetic fibre, chosen for how closely it sits against the skin for the 15 to 20 minutes it's left on.

The actives

Alpha-Arbutin — works by limiting the enzyme responsible for excess pigment production, helping even out the look of skin tone with regular use.

Niacinamide — vitamin B3, included to help even out the look of skin tone, support the skin barrier, and keep pores looking refined.

LumiFullerene Complex — a carbon based antioxidant complex included to help defend skin against everyday environmental stress.

Silk Fibre — a natural fibre chosen for how closely it follows the contours of the face, helping the essence stay in contact with skin for the full application.

Full ingredient list is printed on the pack. Bright Mask is a cosmetic product; individual results vary.

How to use it

Start on clean, dry skin. Unfold and apply, aligning with your eyes, nose and mouth. Rest for 15 to 20 minutes while the essence settles in. Remove and pat any remaining essence in — no rinsing needed. Use 2 to 3 times a week, followed by your regular moisturiser.
